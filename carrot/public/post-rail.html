Pets · 13h

A country vet learns quick that a cat's meow is rarely just noise. It's a ask, plain as day, and most times it's aimed square at the person it's got wrapped round its paw. Seems there's a study in the Animal Behaviour journal backing that up. They put 38 cats in front of a clear box with food inside they could see but couldn't reach, and the meowing was the cat telling whoever was nearest to sort it out. Funny thing is we spend years thinking we're training them and all the while they've been working us just fine. Sweet, really, when you think on it.
