Four ways a watched office can show the same kind of item. The person does not write it. The link is the paper or the record, never the Google News wrapper.

Example 1

The paper

One sentence says what happened. The card is Newsweek: the picture, the paper, and the article’s own headline. The link opens Newsweek.

Jon Ossoff · 14m

A bill to create a new national park moved a step ahead.

Newsweek
America just got one step closer to a new national park
